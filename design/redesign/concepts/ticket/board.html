Concept board · redesign round 2 · slug ticket

Ton ticket s’imprime.

Le site entier est un ticket de caisse qui s’imprime pour toi, ligne à ligne : où, ouvert, combien, quand, avec qui. Réserver, c’est construire ton ticket ; le total ne quitte jamais le bas du papier ; au bout, un tampon rouge : Réservé.

How the seed was twisted. The ticket is not a nostalgic prop and never a texture: it is a width. One column of paper, 80 mm wide at every screen size, laid on a dark counter, and the photographs live under the paper, not on it. The « printer » is one type family in three widths (condensed, normal, double-width) instead of a monospace costume. The only humour is in the words, and the only red is stamp ink.

Sept réponses, sept lignes.

The ticket’s head answers the seven street questions before anything scrolls: where, open now, how much, how soon, is it good, phone, directions. The photograph is the counter it lies on; the paper slides up over it as it prints.

02 · La réservation

Construis ton ticket.

Not a bottom sheet: a page that is the ticket itself. Choices sit on the grey tray above; the paper is pinned at the foot and never exceeds a quarter of the screen. Each choice prints one more line onto it, and the total is always the last line before the button. The deep link (?reserver&barbier=frank&prestation=coupe-classique, codes or readable names) pre-prints the lines; Back and Esc tear the page off and return to the home with focus on « Réserver ».

05 · Identité

Papier, encre, un rouge.

Nothing is textured. The paper is a cool white, not a cream; the ink is near-black with a touch of brown; the red is rubber-stamp ink, taken from the piping of the shop’s own sofas and the LED under the mirror, cooled down from the current LED red so that it reads as print.

Condensed caps for the labels · Où · Avec · Total

One grotesque drawn for newsprint, with a real width axis. Condensed for the labels (a printer’s small mode), normal for the lines, double-width heavy for the figures and the headlines (a printer’s double-width mode). Tabular figures everywhere, so prices line up down the ticket. Not a monospace: the ticket is an idea, not a costume.

Allerta Stencil · the stamp and the wordmark only

Réservé

L’Architecte

An architect’s drawing is approved with a stencil stamp (« Bon pour exécution »). This is the shop’s name made literal: the one place where the identity says architecte without drawing a blueprint. Used at two sizes only, never for text.

Fallbacks: Archivo → Archivo Narrow, Helvetica Neue, Arial · Allerta Stencil → Stencil, Impact. Self-hosted in the build (R10).

The four photographs : printed in ink, under the paper

Grayscale, a touch more contrast, never cropped to a mood: the paper covers what it covers. Each one is a gap between two segments of the ticket, with its price on the next line. Sequence on the home: fade (hero), sidepart, curls, salon (at the stub, behind « 16, place Pélabon »).
