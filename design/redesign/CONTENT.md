# The shop's real content

Everything a designer may use, taken from the running demo salon and the live site's own copy. **Nothing here may be invented, rounded or « improved »**: prices, hours, names, sentences. The demo's numbers stand in for the live catalogue (Comptoir › Catalogue); the structure is what is real. French is the site's language and *tu* is its voice (see `design/programme/07-lexicon.fr.md`).

## The booking rules (real, from the settings)

Slots every 30 min, bookable up to 90 days ahead, at least 30 min notice. A chosen slot is **held 5 minutes**. **Deposit**: services from 50 € (Transformation, Silver, …) ask 30 % up front (15 € on the Transformation), deducted at the salon, **refunded if cancelled more than 12 h ahead**; it must be paid within 15 minutes or the slot is released. A no-show fee of 10 € is configured. A waiting list (« Me prévenir si une place se libère ») and a live walk-in wait exist. No account is needed: a first name and a phone number are enough. The confirmation hands back the day and hour, the barber, the address, the SMS text word for word, a calendar file, directions, « Déplacer » and « Annuler ».
